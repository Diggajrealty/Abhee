Project Overview

The Royal Living, Ready Today

Abhee King's Court (often searched as "Abhee Kings Court") is a completed, ready-to-move apartment community by Abhee Ventures at Chembenahalli Village, Sarjapura Hobli, off Sarjapur Road in south-east Bengaluru. The project has 116 apartments in two blocks (A & B) of a 9-storey building, with basement and stilt parking, a modern elevation and balconies in every flat.

Around 70% of the site is open area, given over to a swimming pool, landscaped gardens, children's play zones, a multi-purpose court and a jogging track, alongside a clubhouse with a party hall, indoor gym and indoor games. Homes come as 2, 2.5 and 3 BHK layouts, with a high UDS (undivided share of land) allocation, wooden-finished flooring in the master bedroom and plans arranged for continuous natural light.

Because the building is ready, you can walk through the exact flat you are considering before you commit. It suits families and tech professionals who want to live on the Sarjapur Road corridor, close to top international schools and within reach of the Wipro SEZ and other IT campuses.

How the Abhee King's Court price is calculated

The developer's October 2026 cost sheet prices every flat at a flat rate of ₹9,500 per sq. ft. of super built-up area (SBA). To estimate the base price of any unit:

Base price = Super built-up area (sq. ft.) × ₹9,500

Example: flat B506 is 1,530 sq. ft. super built-up, so 1,530 × ₹9,500 = ₹1,45,35,000. The 3 BHK A902 at 1,720 sq. ft. works out to ₹1,63,40,000.

Not included in the ₹9,500 rate – budget separately for:

  • Stamp duty and registration charges (statutory)
  • Advance maintenance
  • Corpus / sinking fund
  • GST, if applicable

Ask us for the official cost sheet for the flat you like – it shows each of these line items, so you can see the full amount payable before you book. Prices are subject to availability.
